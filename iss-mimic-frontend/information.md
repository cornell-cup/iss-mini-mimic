EXPLANATION

ECLSS
Environmental Control & Life Support System

Oxygen Generation
Produces oxygen for crew using OGS Racks.
This is part of Water Recovery & Management.

Water Recovery
Provides water by reclaiming wastewater using WRS Racks to make potable water for the crew.

Atmosphere Control & Supply
Provides cabin atmosphere pressure control, nitrogen and oxygen distribution.

Atmosphere Revitalization
Cleans circulating cabin air and removes carbon dioxide using CDRA.

Temp & Humidity Control
Maintains temperature and humidity levels.
Circulates air between modules.
